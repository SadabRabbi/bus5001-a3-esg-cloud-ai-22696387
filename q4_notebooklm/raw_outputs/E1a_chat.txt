According to the University of Melbourne Sustainability Report 2022, the University has established two primary carbon and greenhouse gas emissions targets along with specific progress milestones[1]:
1. Emissions Targets and Deadlines

* 2025 Carbon Neutral Target: The University target is to be certified carbon neutral by 2025 under Climate Active standards across its operations[1].
* 2030 Climate Positive Target: The University target is to achieve climate positive status by 2030[3]. This commitment extends to including its entire Investment Portfolio within its Scope 3 emissions reporting and climate positive targets by 2030[5][6].

2. Reported Progress Against These Targets (in 2022)
Comprehensive Baseline & Inventory Expansion

* Commissioned carbon specialists Pangolin Associates to create a comprehensive, Climate Active–compliant Greenhouse Gas Inventory (GHGI) for 2022 to establish the baseline needed to achieve the 2025 carbon neutral target[2][7].
* Expanded Scope 3 tracking by incorporating the University's entire procurement spend of over $1 billion across all purchasing categories[7], as well as staff working-from-home (WFH) emissions (2,313 tCO₂-e) for the first time[8].
* Total reported net greenhouse gas emissions for 2022 stood at 164,904 tCO₂-e[9].

Electricity & Renewable Energy

* Achieved zero net emissions from electricity for the second consecutive year in 2022[2][10].
* Sourced 64% of total electricity consumption from renewable sources by voluntarily retiring 49,575 Large-scale Generation Certificates (LGCs), primarily sourced from wind farm Power Purchase Agreements (Murra Warra and Crowlands)[2][11].
* Retired carbon offsets covering the residual electricity emissions of approximately 39,000 tCO₂-e[9].
* Generated over 3 GWh of clean energy annually on campus from more than 11,000 solar PV panels across 44 buildings[12].

Electrification & Campus Energy Upgrades

* Invested $6 million in Smart Campus Energy Upgrades (LED lighting, chiller upgrades, solar PV, and HVAC optimization), delivering expected electricity savings of 3.8 GWh per year[10][12].
* Began a long-term electrification program to replace natural gas infrastructure with electric systems, completing pilot projects that replaced 13 gas hot-water units across 10 buildings with electric heat pumps and scoping full electrification for the Burnley campus[4].

Business Travel & Fleet Emissions

* Fully offset business flight emissions of 15,581 tCO₂-e[9].
* Offsets totaling 754 tCO₂-e were purchased through Greenfleet for vehicle fleet emissions (315 tCO₂-e)[9][11].

Investment Portfolio

* Established a baseline for equity investments, measuring combined Australian and International Equities carbon emissions at 95,649 tCO₂-e, which performed below the total benchmark of 109,066 tCO₂-e[13][14].
