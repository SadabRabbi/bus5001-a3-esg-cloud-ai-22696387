According to the RMIT 2024 Sustainability Annual Report, the University details the following regarding its renewable onshore grid electricity:
1. Share of Onshore Grid Electricity That Is Renewable

* Share: **100%**[1][2].
* Exact Quote: The report notes that RMIT *"maintained its 100% onshore grid electricity supplied by renewable sources"*[1] and confirms that *"From 1 January 2023, all onshore grid electricity was sourced from renewable energy"*[2].

2. How the Electricity Is Sourced
The report states that this renewable electricity is sourced through:

* Wind Farm Partnerships: Supplied *"through our wind farm partnerships in regional Victoria"*[1].
* Melbourne Renewable Energy Projects (MREP): The *"Melbourne Renewable Energy Projects 1 & 2 supplied 50,107MWh of renewable electricity to RMIT in 2024"*[2].
