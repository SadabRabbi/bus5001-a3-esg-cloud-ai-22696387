# Ethics and privacy
- Only public sustainability reports published by universities were uploaded to NotebookLM. They contain no personal data.
- No confidential, student, staff or client data was used.
- The repository stores links to the sources, not copies of the PDFs, to respect publisher copyright.
- The 39.7 MB audio file produced by NotebookLM is not stored here; a transcript supplied by the student is.
- Provider data-handling terms were not reviewed. Do not upload confidential material without approval from the organisation's privacy owner.
- Screenshots were cropped to remove account details where needed. CONFIRM before upload that no personal photo or email address is visible.
